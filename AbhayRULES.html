Welcome to the Server

Please review our community guidelines below to ensure a safe and fun environment for everyone.

Join Our Discord Subscribe on YouTube
1

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness. Bullying, harassment, or toxicity of any kind will not be tolerated.

2

No Spamming

Avoid flooding chat channels with excessive messages, emojis, images, or mentions. Keep the chat readable.

3

Keep It Appropriate

Ensure all conversations and media remain safe for a general audience. No offensive or inappropriate content.

4

Follow Discord Rules

Respect and abide by all official Discord Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

5

No Hate Speech

Discrimination, racism, sexism, or hate speech targeting any group or individual is strictly forbidden.

6

No Advertising

Do not promote other Discord servers, social media links, or products without explicit permission from the staff.

7

Use Channels Properly

Keep your conversations relevant to the specific topic of each channel.

8

No Fake Information

Do not spread rumors, misinformation, or lies that could cause confusion or drama in the community.

9

Respect Admins & Mods

Follow instructions given by the staff. If you have an issue with a decision, DM a moderator privately.

10

Keep Language Clean

Avoid excessive swearing or coarse language to ensure the environment remains welcoming to everyone.

11

No NSFW Content

Not Safe For Work (NSFW) material, including explicit text or media, is completely banned.

12

Protect Privacy

Do not share anyone’s personal information, real names, or private photos without their explicit consent.

13

No Impersonation

Do not pretend to be other members, staff, or public figures by copying their usernames or avatars.

14

Stay Positive

Help maintain a friendly and constructive community. Treat others the way you want to be treated.

15

Follow All Rules

Breaking these guidelines will result in moderation actions, which may include mutes, kicks, or permanent bans.

"RULES are not a burden. It’s a way of manner to Respect others and live Peacefully."
